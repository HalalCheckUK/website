Halal Check UK – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Halal Check UK ("the app", "we") helps UK shoppers check whether a food product looks halal by scanning its barcode or reading its ingredients. The app is published by The One+ Apps. This page explains, in plain English, what information the app handles, why, and how you can delete it.

Contact

Questions or requests about your information: halalcheckuk@gmail.com

No account needed

The app has no sign-in. Scanning, checking ingredients, saving products and tagging supermarkets all work without an account, and your history stays on your phone only.

What we collect

  • Products you save and supermarkets you tag: when you save a product from an ingredients photo, or tell us where you found a product, this is shared with other shoppers. It is not linked to your name or email. To limit misuse, it carries a random ID created by the app on your phone, which is not linked to your name or email.
  • Your answers to "Is this result right?": other people only see the totals. A vote carries the same random ID, which is not linked to your name or email.
  • Photos of ingredient labels: a photo you take is sent through our server to an AI service (Anthropic) only to read the ingredients text. We do not keep the photo. The text that is read may be saved as part of a product if you choose to save it.
  • Barcode lookups: when you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to our database and to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a public food database, to find the product.
  • Technical information: like any online service, our hosting provider and servers may receive your IP address and basic request details when the app contacts them. We use this only to run and protect the service, for example to limit abuse.
  • Camera: the app asks for camera permission to scan barcodes and photograph ingredient labels. Photos are not stored on our servers.
  • On your phone: to run faster and use less data, the app keeps a small temporary store of recent product lookups on your phone.

Why we use it

To check products for you, to show other shoppers which products are in which supermarkets, and to prevent abuse of the service.

Services we use

  • Supabase – hosts our database and server functions.
  • Anthropic – the AI service that reads ingredient text from label photos.
  • Open Food Facts – public product data and pictures.
  • Expo / EAS – used to build and deliver app updates.

These providers process information on our behalf and under their own privacy terms.

No selling, no ads

We never sell your information. We do not show adverts and we do not use your information for advertising.

How long we keep it

Your history is kept on your phone until you clear it or uninstall the app. Shared products, supermarket tags and vote totals are kept to help other shoppers; they are not linked to your name or email.

Your choices and how to delete your data

  • Clear history: in the app go to History, then "Clear history". This removes your history from your phone.
  • Other requests: you can ask to see, correct or delete information we hold about you by emailing halalcheckuk@gmail.com. Under UK data protection law you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Not religious or medical advice

Results are a guide based on ingredient information. Always check the packaging, and ask a qualified person if you need a ruling or have an allergy.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
